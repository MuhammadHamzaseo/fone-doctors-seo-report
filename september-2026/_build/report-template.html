Google removes pages on its own schedule, not ours. The remaining broken URLs — 83 were still visible in the 28-day data — are submitted for removal and will drop out over the coming weeks. This work continues into next month until the index contains only real, valuable pages.

⚠️ Why removing pages helps rankings. It sounds backwards — fewer pages, better results? But Google assesses a site as a whole. Thousands of broken or duplicate pages tell it the site is poorly maintained, and they soak up the crawl attention that should go to the pages that matter. Every junk page removed makes the remaining pages look stronger. This is one of the highest-impact technical jobs in SEO and it is rarely done properly.

10 — Microsoft Clarity · Visitor behaviour

Microsoft Clarity: what people do once they arrive 🎯

Microsoft Clarity is now installed and tracking on fonedoctors.co.uk. Google tells us who arrives; Clarity tells us what they do next. It records every session, detects meaningful actions automatically, and lets us watch real visitor recordings and heatmaps — and this month it tracked 176 high-intent actions: checkouts started, forms submitted, bookings, contact requests and quote requests.

🔬 What Microsoft Clarity is, and why we installed it. Clarity is Microsoft's free behaviour analytics tool. Unlike a traffic counter, it shows how people use the site — where they click, how far they scroll, where they hesitate, and which actions they complete. Its "smart events" automatically detect purchases, form submissions and bookings without any manual setup. This is the data that turns rankings into revenue decisions: we can now see exactly which pages lead to a booking and which ones lose people.

Reference

The terms used in this report 📖

ImpressionsThe number of times the website appeared in Google search results, whether or not anyone clicked.
ClicksThe number of times someone clicked through from Google to the website.
Average positionWhere the site typically appears in results. Position 1 is the top. Positions 1–10 are page one.
Click-through rate (CTR)Clicks divided by impressions. The share of people who saw the listing and chose it.
Non-brand searchesSearches that do not include the business name. Made by people who do not yet know the business — the real growth audience.
Indexed pagesPages Google has stored and can show in results. Fewer, better pages beat thousands of junk pages.
Schema / structured dataCode that tells Google exactly what a page is — an article, an FAQ — so it can show rich features in results.
Rich resultsEnhanced Google listings with extra features like FAQ boxes, star ratings or images. They earn more clicks.
BacklinkA link from another website to yours. Google treats them as votes of confidence.
Spam scoreAn estimate of how risky a site's incoming links look. Lower is better. 1% is the lowest band.
Internal linkA link from one page on the site to another. It passes ranking strength and helps Google understand structure.
Smart eventsMicrosoft Clarity's automatic detection of meaningful visitor actions — checkouts, form submissions, bookings.
