Cleaning the link profile to lower spam score

We're working through the list of low-quality backlinks and submitting removals so Google discounts them. At the same time we're building a clean, relevant backlink profile — business directories, quality profiles and local citations — to replace weak links with trustworthy ones. Why this protects your rankings

A high spam score signals risk to Google and can hold back an otherwise healthy site. Removing harmful links and replacing them with quality ones is like repairing a credit history — it takes consistent effort, but it's essential for long-term ranking stability and trust. It's slow, unglamorous, and one of the highest-value protective jobs in SEO.

12 — Plain English

Every SEO Term, Explained Simply

So nothing in this report is a mystery — here's every term used, in everyday language.

SEO
Search Engine Optimisation — the work of helping a website get found on Google.
Impressions
How many times your site appeared in Google search results, whether or not it was clicked.
Clicks
How many people actually clicked through to your website from Google.
Position / Ranking
Where your page sits in the search results. #1 is the top; page one is roughly the top 10.
CTR
Click-Through Rate — the percentage of people who clicked after seeing your listing.
Keyword
The word or phrase a customer types into Google, e.g. "phone repair london".
Search Console
Google's free tool that shows exactly how your site performs in search.
Canonical tag
A behind-the-scenes tag telling Google the correct, official address of a page.
Schema / Structured data
Hidden code that explains your content to Google, enabling star ratings & FAQs in results.
Backlink
A link from another website to yours — Google treats it like a vote of confidence.
Spam score
An estimate of how risky or low-quality a site's link profile looks. Lower is better.
Sitemap
A file listing your important pages so Google can find and crawl them.
Indexing
When Google stores your page in its database so it can appear in search.
Topical authority
Proving to Google you're a genuine expert on a subject by covering it thoroughly.
